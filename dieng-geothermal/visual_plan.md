# Visual plan: "They Ran From the Volcano" (English global v2)

## 5 visual styles

| Code | Style | Look | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CIN** | Cinematic real | Photoreal close-ups, natural light, slightly desaturated | Hook (#1–#3): the "real world" before we enter the model world |
| **MAP** | Minimal 3D Relief Map | Matte white plaster terrain, pale blue ocean, thin grid, miniature volcano with 3D smoke, small white figurines | Places & the world: Dieng, Ring of Fire, world ranking, Lake Nyos, villager scenes |
| **CORE** | Cinematic Earth Layers Cutaway | Dark space, glowing concentric layers, vertical guide line with label cards | The "dive" into the Earth (#6–#8) |
| **BLOCK** | Geological Block Diagram | Floating terrain cube, cross-section with rock layers, reservoir, wells; same cream/blue palette as MAP | Mechanism: pressure cooker, gas (#9–#21) |
| **GFX** | Remotion motion graphics | Clean vector overlays: gauges, arrows, gas outlines, icons, text | Overlays on any style + text-only beats |

Rule: **all text and numbers are added in Remotion**, never generated by AI.

## 4 animation techniques

| Code | Technique | Tool | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T2V** | Text-to-video, 4–5 s clip | AI video (Veo / Kling / Gemini Omni via ComfyUI, ElevenLabs or Higgsfield) | Highest: use only 3× |
| **I2V** | AI still image → image-to-video (smoke, slow dolly) | AI image + AI video | Medium |
| **2.5D** | AI still image moved in Remotion (pan, zoom, parallax, "dive") | AI image + Remotion | Low (one image, many shots) |
| **OVL** | Animated vector overlay on top of a still or clip | Remotion | Free |
