## 4–5. Prompt gambar & animasi (Omni Flash 1.2, text-to-video, 9:16, 1080p)
Gaya tetap untuk semua adegan: *cinematic historical paper diorama, layered textured paper with visible fibres and cut edges, soft drop shadows, pop-up book parallax, tilt-shift depth of field, warm volumetric light, teal–jade–gold–crimson palette, film grain, premium documentary look, no text, no voice.* Frame terakhir adegan 1 dipakai sebagai referensi gaya untuk adegan 2–5.

- **Adegan 1 (sudah dibuat, 6 s → 3 s):** Extreme close-up: a single paper tea leaf drifts down through golden dust and lands in a blue-and-white porcelain cup with a paper ripple. Camera pulls back fast and rises: the cup is the centre of a multi-tier pop-up diorama — Imperial Chinese palace on top, European aristocrats with teacups in the middle, a humble roadside tea stall at the bottom.
- **Adegan 2:** Ancient Chinese emperor Shen Nong in paper robes sits under a paper tea tree beside a boiling cauldron; a breeze carries leaves into the water; rising steam curls into the character 茶. Slow push-in.
- **Adegan 3:** Paper tea bricks pressed and traded by Tibetan merchants on a mountain trail, then a 3D paper map where a Dutch sailing ship travels from Asia to Europe along a glowing line, ending in an aristocratic European salon pouring tea.
- **Adegan 4:** Foggy night, a British botanist disguised in Chinese robes carries a glass Wardian case of tea seedlings; cut to a paper map with a red arrow from China to India and a wax seal labelled monopoly cracking apart. Tense, low-key lighting.
- **Adegan 5:** A golden imperial teacup match-cuts into a tall glass of Indonesian iced sweet tea on a wooden warung table; ice cubes glint; paper folds open to reveal an end-card space.

## Catatan fakta
- Shen Nong 2737 SM adalah legenda, dan narasi menyebutnya legenda.
- Bata teh dipakai sebagai alat tukar di Tibet, Mongolia, dan Siberia.
- Teh dibawa pedagang Belanda ke Eropa pada awal abad ke-17.
- Robert Fortune (1848–1851) menyamar dan membawa bibit & tanaman teh dari Tiongkok ke India untuk East India Company. Teh Assam juga ditemukan secara terpisah di India, jadi "monopoli runtuh" adalah penyederhanaan dari runtuhnya dominasi Tiongkok atas pasokan teh.
- Teh umum disebut minuman paling banyak dikonsumsi setelah air.
